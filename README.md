# Adhithyan M. — Personal Portfolio

A bespoke, editorial-grade personal portfolio built with **React**, **Vite**, and **Tailwind CSS**.

---

## 🎨 Design System

- **Palette**: Deep Navy (`#0A1226`, `#050B17`), Warm Paper Off-White (`#FCFCF9`, `#F7F7F3`), and Subtle Teal (`#14B8A6`).
- **Typography**: Playfair Display (Editorial Serif), Plus Jakarta Sans (Interface Sans), and JetBrains Mono (Technical Monospace).
- **Layout**: Inspired by high-end publication design — volume markers, hairline dividers, structured dossiers, and focused reading ergonomics.

---

## 🧭 How to Customize Your Content (Zero Coding Required!)

All the text on the website is centralized in one single file:
👉 **`src/data/portfolioData.js`**

Open that file in any text editor and edit the text inside the quotes `""`:
1. **Personal Information**: Name, title, email, city, and social links.
2. **About Me**: Your bio paragraphs, foundational pillars, and background facts.
3. **Experience & Education**: Your actual job titles, companies, dates, and milestones.
4. **Selected Projects**: Your case study titles, challenges, architectural solutions, and outcomes.
5. **Skills**: Your specific tools, frameworks, and proficiency ratings.
6. **Ideas & Insights**: Your essays, articles, or research notes.

Whenever you save `src/data/portfolioData.js`, the website will automatically update in your browser instantly.

## 🚀 Running Locally

To view the website in your browser:
- Open: **[http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/)**

To stop the server:
- Press `Ctrl + C` in your terminal.

To start it again anytime:
```bash
npm run dev
```
